Hi, I'm Heidi 👋
I share the same name as *Heidi, Girl of the Alps*, and I guess I’m also someone who loves being outdoors.

I'm currently a Front-End Engineer based in Tokyo.

If I had to describe myself in a few words: I enjoy taking on challenges, I’m naturally curious, and I take responsibility for the choices I make.
I love cats, traveling, and exploring new things.

My journey into programming started from something simple — I just wanted to build a travel blog.
I began learning through online courses, and later joined the Lidemy Mentor Program, where I gradually found the direction I wanted to pursue.

For me, these activities are different ways of understanding myself and exploring how I want to live.

This blog is where I document my journey — from frontend and backend development, to outdoor experiences, and life in Japan.
